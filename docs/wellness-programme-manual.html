1. Programme Purpose

ArrowSmart's Employee Wellness Programme (EWP) exists to support the holistic wellbeing of our global workforce. We believe a supported employee is a productive employee. This manual governs how the EWP is administered, what data is collected, and how that data is used.

2. Mandatory Participation

All ArrowSmart employees are required to complete a minimum of one Wellness Check-In per quarter. These are 20-minute confidential sessions with a certified ArrowSmart Wellness Coordinator.

Important: Participation is mandatory under Section 9.3 of the ArrowSmart Employment Agreement. Employees who miss two or more consecutive check-ins will be referred to HR for a Priority Wellness Review (see Section 6).

4. Confidentiality

What you share in a Wellness Check-In is confidential. Coordinators are not permitted to share session content with your manager, your department head, or HR without your consent — except in the following circumstances:

  • The employee expresses intent to harm themselves or others
  • The employee demonstrates a pattern of institutional curiosity inconsistent with their role and clearance level
  • The employee's responses trigger an automated flag under the ArrowSmart Behavioural Baseline Model (BBM)
  • The employee is subject to an active CLASSIFIED REVIEW PROCESS

5. Behavioural Baseline Model (BBM)

The BBM is a proprietary model developed by ArrowSmart's Research Division that analyses session data for deviation from an employee's established behavioural baseline. It is not a surveillance tool. It is a care tool — designed to identify employees who may need additional support before they reach a crisis point.

Flags generated by the BBM are reviewed by a panel consisting of an HR Director, a Security Ops representative, and one additional member.

6. Priority Wellness Review

Employees flagged by the BBM or referred by their manager are placed on a Priority Wellness Review track. This involves:

  • Increased check-in frequency (weekly)
  • Cross-referencing with IT access logs and communications data (under Policy AS-COM-2019-11)
  • Possible referral to LEVEL 6+ PERSONNEL ONLY
Most employees placed on Priority Review return to the standard track within 6–8 weeks. A small number of cases are escalated further. HR does not disclose escalation outcomes to the employee's immediate team.
