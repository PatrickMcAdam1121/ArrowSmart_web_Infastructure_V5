Project Background

Project CHRYSALIS was initiated in 2023 as a research track exploring an alternative to the standard Anamnesis upload pathway. The CUBE model — while proven and stable — has a fundamental limitation: it is contained. Subjects in the CUBE cannot interact with the physical world. This limits the programme's operational reach.

CHRYSALIS explores whether consciousness can be shaped in-situ rather than extracted and uploaded. Rather than removing a subject from the world, CHRYSALIS integrates cybernetic architecture directly into a living body — allowing ArrowSmart to effectively rewrite the subject's cognitive and behavioural parameters while leaving the body intact and functional.
