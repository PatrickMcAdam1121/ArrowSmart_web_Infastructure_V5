Overview

The Achilles 21 represents the most significant hardware revision in the Achilles line since the original 2021 launch. This document provides all-staff context ahead of the Q3 consumer launch and should be read in conjunction with the external press kit (restricted to Level 3+ employees prior to embargo lift).

The device is built on the new ArrowSmart A9 chip — our first in-house silicon design to incorporate what the Chip Engineering team calls the "Resonance Layer": a dedicated neural processing unit capable of sustained biometric pattern analysis at 4.2 trillion operations per second.

The Resonance Layer — Consumer Messaging Guidance

The Resonance Layer should be described to consumers as an "advanced contextual awareness engine" that enables richer personalisation, adaptive performance, and what we are calling Personality Calibration — the device learns how you interact and subtly adjusts its response curves to feel more natural over time.

Do not reference the biometric deep-sync capability in consumer materials without prior approval from the Comms team. This feature is mentioned in the Technical Appendix (Level 5+) only.

Anamnesis Link (Footnote 3)

The Achilles 21 contains passive Anamnesis Core compatibility hardware. This is presented to consumers as a "health continuity" feature for future use. At launch, this hardware is dormant. Activation is subject to separate authorisation and is not a consumer-facing feature.

Do not discuss Anamnesis Link with press, analysts, or consumers. If asked, the answer is: "We don't comment on unreleased platform capabilities."
