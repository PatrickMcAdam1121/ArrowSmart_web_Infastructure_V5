2. Subject Classification

Each subject is assigned one of four classifications upon intake:

  • Standard — Full Anamnesis wipe, full CUBE upload. Proxy deployed.
  • Partial — Targeted episodic wipe only. Used for subjects whose memory architecture is too complex for full wipe without degradation.
  • Observation — Subject is uploaded but not fully isolated. Used for research cases.
  • Chrysalis — CLASSIFIED — L6+ ONLY

3. Standard Monitoring Protocols

Automated monitoring cycles run every 90 minutes under standard conditions (Protocol Alpha-3). Pod Cluster D is currently operating under Protocol Delta-7 (15-minute cycles) due to elevated variance indicators in Pods D-01 through D-04.

5. Resonance Bleed Detection

Resonance bleed is the term used when a subject's pre-wipe episodic memory begins to surface in detectable form within the CUBE simulation layer. It is exceptionally rare and has only been formally documented in three prior cases.

Indicators include:

  • Rhythmic neural spike patterns at fixed intervals (typically 11 seconds — the significance of this interval is CLASSIFIED — L7+)
  • Spontaneous sensory memory fragments appearing in the subject's CUBE experience
  • Subject expressing anomalous philosophical concern about the nature of their environment
  • Corresponding behavioural drift in the subject's deployed Proxy
Critical Note: Resonance bleed in an active subject is classified as an Awakening Risk event. Do not attempt memory adjustment without authorisation from Dr. Anand's team. Unilateral adjustment carries risk of CLASSIFIED — L8+.

6. Emergency Protocols

6.1 — Pod Life Support Failure

In the event of life support failure, the pod enters emergency preservation mode (72-hour battery backup). Contact the on-call Anamnesis technician immediately via the Sub-Level emergency line (x9911). Do not attempt to manually override pod hardware.

6.2 — Subject Awakening Event

A subject awakening event is defined as the subject gaining verifiable conscious awareness of their stasis state. This has never been recorded. In the event it occurs, immediately trigger PROTOCOL OMEGA — CLASSIFIED.

7. Memory Adjustment Procedures

Memory adjustment (colloquially referred to as a "re-wipe" by technical staff — do not use this term in documentation) is a targeted intervention in which specific neural pathways responsible for anomalous memory activity are suppressed or overwritten.

All memory adjustment procedures require dual authorisation from a Level 7+ officer and Dr. Anand's division. Procedures are performed remotely via the Anamnesis Core adjustment interface in Sub-Level 4, Lab C.
