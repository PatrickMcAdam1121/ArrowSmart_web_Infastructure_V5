1. System Overview

The Anamnesis Core is a three-layer computational system designed to perform three functions:

  1. Memory Extraction & Archiving — Capture, digitise, and store the complete episodic memory architecture of a biological subject
  2. Memory Wipe & Suppression — Selectively or completely erase episodic memory from a living brain using targeted electromagnetic and chemical protocols
  3. Consciousness Upload — Transfer the digitised memory architecture to the CUBE simulation environment, establishing a persistent and subjectively continuous digital existence

The system operates on the fundamental principle that consciousness is a pattern, not a substance. What makes you you is the structure of your memories and the relationships between them — not the biological material that holds them. The Anamnesis Core reads that pattern, archives it, and — with the biological material cleared — writes it into silicon.

2. Layer 1 — Extraction Engine

2.1 Neural Mapping Array

The extraction process begins with full neural mapping. The subject is placed in the extraction chair (Sub-Level 4, Bay 1) and connected to the 4,096-point neural array. The array achieves a 98.7% capture resolution of active neural pathways. The remaining 1.3% consists of deep limbic structures that resist mapping — these are approximated using our proprietary interpolation model (ANAM-INTERP-v3).

Extraction takes between 4 and 11 hours depending on subject complexity. During extraction, the subject is sedated and experiences no pain. They are told they are receiving an MRI.

2.2 Memory Topology Graph

The raw neural capture is processed into a Memory Topology Graph (MTG) — a mathematical model of the subject's memory architecture. The MTG is the actual unit that is uploaded to the CUBE. It contains:

  • All episodic memories (with temporal relationships)
  • Semantic knowledge structures
  • Emotional weighting maps
  • Personality trait vectors
  • Self-model (the subject's understanding of who they are)

3. Layer 2 — Wipe Protocol

The memory wipe is performed in two stages:

STAGE 1: Targeted electromagnetic pulse array
  — Disrupts active memory consolidation pathways
  — Duration: 45 minutes
  — Irreversible above threshold level

STAGE 2: Chemical suppression (intrathecal injection)
  — Compound: [CLASSIFIED — L9+]
  — Prevents new episodic memory formation for 72 hours
  — Provides window for Proxy deployment

Following wipe, the subject's brain retains procedural memory (motor function, language, learned skills) but has no access to episodic or autobiographical memory. From the subject's perspective: they wake up not knowing who they are, but still knowing how to be human.

A cover narrative is provided by HR. The standard script is: "You were in an accident. You've had some memory loss. Your family has been notified. Let's focus on your recovery."

4. Layer 3 — CUBE Upload & Instantiation

The MTG is transferred to the target CUBE sector and instantiated as a full simulation instance. The subject's digital consciousness believes it is experiencing an uninterrupted continuation of its life — in a slightly different location, with slightly different routines, on a day that feels like any other day.

The CUBE's environmental simulation is detailed enough to be indistinguishable from physical reality at all sensory resolutions the human brain can perceive. The subject does not know they are in a simulation. They never have reason to suspect it.

They are, to every standard of measurement we have, alive.
