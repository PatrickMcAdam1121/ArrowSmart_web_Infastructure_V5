Our Story

Moving forward.
Always pointing up.

For four decades, ArrowSmart has been at the absolute forefront of consumer technology. We don't follow trends — we define them. Our arrowhead stands for one thing: relentless forward motion.

Four decades of firsts.

1984

ArrowSmart is founded

Founded in a garage in San Mateo, CA, ArrowSmart ships its first personal computer, the Arrow 1, to 12,000 customers in its opening week.

1997

The Arrow OS era begins

ArrowOS launches, unifying the company's software stack and pioneering the vertically integrated computing experience.

2005

The original Achilles phone

ArrowSmart enters the smartphone market with the first Achilles, redefining what a phone could be. 4 million units sold in its first quarter.

2014

AS Silicon — the first proprietary chip

The AS-1 chip ships in the Achilles 10, delivering performance no competitor could match. ArrowSmart becomes the only consumer tech company with full silicon-to-software ownership.

2024

Achilles 20 — two decades of excellence

The Achilles 20 Series launches, powered by the AS-9 chip. 2.4 billion ArrowSmart devices are now active worldwide. The arrow keeps pointing forward.

2026

$412 billion — a new record

ArrowSmart reports FY2025 revenue of $412.1B, its largest year on record. The company reaches 100% renewable energy across all owned facilities and begins construction of its second carbon-neutral campus.

What we stand for

Performance First

Every product we ship is the fastest, most capable device in its class. We don't compromise on performance — ever.

Built to Last

ArrowSmart devices are engineered for longevity. We provide software updates for a minimum of seven years and repair support for ten.

Privacy by Design

Your data belongs to you. ArrowSmart processes sensitive information on-device and has never sold user data to third parties.

Environment

Building a greener future.

Since 2022, 100% of ArrowSmart's owned and operated facilities run on renewable energy. We've committed to carbon neutrality across our entire supply chain by 2030 and to producing zero landfill waste by 2028.
